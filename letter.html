To my lovey,

On our first anniversary.

Sometimes, I wonder what would have become of us had certain things never happened. Would I have been a little gentler? Would loving me have been a little easier for you? I suppose I will never know.

What I do know is that I have been unkind to you. I have brought up things we have already cried over, turned good days into arguments, and made you answer for mistakes you have long apologized for. I am sorry. It is unfair, asking someone to stay while making it difficult for them to do so.

I think a part of me is still afraid. Perhaps I have grown so accustomed to anticipating the next disappointment that I have forgotten how to simply be happy with you. I do not want to live like that anymore, lovey. I do not want to look back years from now and realize that I spent more time remembering how you hurt me than appreciating the ways you loved me.

I want to move past it, and I hope to learn how to do so without betraying myself in the process. I want us to be honest with each other, even when the truth is difficult to bear. I want to give us a chance at the love we have always wanted from one another.

But enough about the things that have hurt us. There is so much about this past year that I want to remember fondly. The anticipation of seeing you again, the conversations I wished would never end, the mundane little things about your day that you thought were hardly worth telling me. I hope you know how much those things mean to me. Even from miles away, you have become such an intimate part of my everyday life.

How wonderful it is to have someone whose existence makes me look forward to the most ordinary things. Grocery shopping together, waking up beside you, deciding what to have for dinner, or coming home after an exhausting day knowing you are there. I find myself longing for a life with you that may seem uneventful to others, but to me, it would be everything.

A year has passed since we became each other's, and there is still so much of you I wish to know. I want to meet every version of you that the coming years will bring. I want to witness the things you have yet to accomplish, celebrate the little victories you might overlook, and be there when life becomes a little too much. I hope I get to love you through all the ages of your life.

I have seen the less beautiful parts of you, just as you have seen mine. There is very little left of the innocence with which we first loved each other.

I will love you nonetheless.

I only hope that as the years pass, I learn to love you more gently than I have. I hope you never have to wonder whether you are still adored by me. I hope there comes a time when the distance between us is merely something we recall from our younger years, when we can laugh about how desperately we once wished to spend an ordinary afternoon together.

Happy first anniversary, my lovey. Thank you for being someone I have had the privilege of loving for an entire year. If life permits, I would like to have many more years of knowing you, of discovering the little things that make you who you are, and of finding new reasons to fall in love with you.

One year with you, and I still find myself wishing that a lifetime were a little longer.

After all, it is still your face I wish to grow old looking at.
