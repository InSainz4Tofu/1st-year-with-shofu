For the Days Between Us

These are for you to keep. Open one whenever you need it, today or any day after.

✉Open when...You miss me+

My lovey,

I wish I could give you more than a message right now. I wish I could sit beside you and let you lean on me for as long as you needed. Until I can, please know that missing you is part of my day, too.

With love, always. ♡

✉Open when...You have had a difficult day+

My lovey,

You do not have to tell me everything immediately, or find the right words for what you feel. If you want to talk, I will listen. If you need some time, take it. Please be gentle with yourself, lovey.

With love, always. ♡

✉Open when...We have had an argument+

My lovey,

I hope we never become so concerned with being right that we forget to be kind to one another. I want to understand you, and I want to be understood, too. When we are both ready, I hope we can talk honestly and find a way through it together.

With love, always. ♡

✉Open when...You are proud of yourself+

My lovey,

Tell me about it, even if you think it is something small. I want to know what made you happy today. I want to be the person you can come to with good news, and I hope you give yourself credit for how far you have come.

With love, always. ♡

✉Open when...You cannot sleep+

My lovey,

I wish I could be there to tell you goodnight without having to put my phone down afterward. For now, I hope you get some proper rest. Whatever tomorrow brings, you do not have to figure it all out tonight.

With love, always. ♡

✉Open when...You are thinking about our first meeting+

My lovey,

I think about that day, too. I wonder how nervous we will be, what we will say, and how different it will feel to finally be within reach of one another. I hope we get to find out soon. Until then, I am looking forward to you.

With love, always. ♡
