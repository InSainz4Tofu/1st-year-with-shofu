The Little Things That Made Me Think Of Her

This page exists for the details that do not always fit cleanly into a letter, but still deserve to be named.

💜Purple

Not just as a favorite color, but as a feeling. A little dreamy, a little soft, and impossible not to notice once it becomes tied to someone you love.

☾Starry Nights

There is something about the night sky that feels made for longing. Quiet enough for honesty, beautiful enough for hope, and wide enough to hold distance without making love feel smaller.

🦫Capybara Comfort

Capybaras somehow make everything feel gentler. They belong beside warm drinks, soft blankets, and the kind of peace I hope we get to share in person one day.

😈Kuromi

A little playful edge, a little charm, and just enough personality to make the whole page feel more hers.

🏠The Ordinary Future

Not a grand fantasy, but the simple life your letter keeps reaching for. Grocery shopping together, deciding on dinner, and coming home to each other after a tiring day.

♡The Part That Stays

Even after fear, distance, and the things that have already hurt you both, there is still that steady wish underneath it all: to love each other better, and to keep choosing each other anyway.

The smallest details are often the most personal. That is why I kept them.
